Help and guidance

Frequently asked questions

What do the access badges (Open Access, Restricted, By Appointment) mean?

Every item shows one of three access conditions. Open Access items can be viewed — and, where rights allow, downloaded — directly. Restricted and By Appointment items require you to contact the holding collection before access is granted.

What do the rights categories mean?

Each item is labelled Public Domain, In Copyright, Request to View, or Cultural Conditions Apply. Public Domain items are free to reuse; In Copyright and Request to View items need permission from the rights holder; Cultural Conditions Apply flags items with additional Indigenous cultural protocols.

How do I ask a question about a specific item?

Open the item's record and use its Contact collection action — it links directly to the museum, archive, or gallery that holds it.

Can I download images or files?

Where an item's rights allow it, a Download action appears on its record. Items marked In Copyright or Request to View require separate permission from the rights holder first.

What does the cultural sensitivity notice mean?

Some items may contain material of cultural significance to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. These are flagged individually — see our Acknowledgement of Country in the footer for our broader commitment to Indigenous knowledge and protocols.

Can I search across every museum, archive, and gallery at once?

Yes — Cultural Collections Search indexes items from all of the University's museums, archives, and galleries in one place. Use the search bar or Explore by theme to search across all of them, or browse a specific collection from the Collections page.

Acknowledgement of Country

The University of Melbourne acknowledge Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people as the Traditional Owners of the unceded lands on which we work, learn and live. We pay respect to Elders past, present and future, and acknowledge the importance of Indigenous knowledge in the Academy.
