Citing Collections

Cite collection items so others can locate the exact record you used. Every item record provides the details you need to build a citation.

1

What to include

A good citation includes the creator, title, date, format, holding collection and the item's persistent identifier. The persistent identifier is the most reliable element because it never changes.

2

Finding the identifier

The persistent identifier is shown on every item's record page, alongside its metadata fields.

3

Example

La Trobe, Charles Joseph. Foundation Charter of the University of Melbourne. 22 January 1853. Parchment document. University Archives, University of Melbourne.
